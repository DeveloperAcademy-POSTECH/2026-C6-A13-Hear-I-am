# Generated illustrations

Created with the built-in image_gen tool, 2026-10-16. The three user-supplied crops were visual references. Assets are bundled in `PoC4/Resources/Assets.xcassets`; no network is needed at runtime. Camera backgrounds use ARKit, not generated pictures.

## StageEmpty

Minimal elegant vector-like raster illustration for Hear I am iOS empty state. Reference image 1 guides composition: front view of a small stage, light grey truss with exactly three spotlights, two speakers, gently outlined audience seats in front; three muted forest-green circular route markers connected with a dashed line on stage. Clean thin charcoal/grey strokes, pale subtle light beams, lots of transparent negative space, no text, no UI, no border. Transparent background, wide 3:2 composition.

## ScanAction

Minimal white line-art illustration for Hear I am black action card. Reference image guides subject: upright portrait smartphone above a four-corner floor rectangle seen in perspective, small white corner dots and short dashed scanning rays, five short white rays above phone. Pure white and pale grey strokes, no dark background baked in, transparent background. No text, no UI, no lettering, centered composition with padding, crisp rounded strokes readable at 120px wide.

## BeltAction

Minimal charcoal black line-art illustration for Hear I am light action card. Reference guides subject: cropped torso outline wearing a horizontal waist belt, upright smartphone attached at center in front of abdomen, small curved haptic waves left and right. No face or legs, no text, no UI, rounded black strokes, white phone screen, transparent background. Centered composition ample padding, crisp and readable at 120px wide.
